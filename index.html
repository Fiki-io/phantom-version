Phantom - Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Summary: Phantom is designed with privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data. Your watch history, subscriptions, and bookmarks stay 100% locally on your device.

1. Overview & Architecture

Phantom ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a seamless, client-side video playback client for Android. We respect user privacy and operate without mandatory user accounts, registration, or analytics trackers.

2. Data Handled by the Application

A. Local Device Data:

  • Watch & Search History: Stored strictly in a local SQLite/Room database on your device sandbox. It is never transmitted to our servers and can be wiped anytime via Settings.
  • Subscriptions & Saved Playlists: Maintained solely on your device without requiring a Google Account login.
  • Preferences: User settings (audio-only playback, sleep timer, resolution choices) are kept in local SharedPreferences.

B. Third-Party Network Services:

  • YouTube / Streaming Endpoints: Search queries and media playback streams are retrieved directly from public streaming endpoints. Use of these services is subject to Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
  • SponsorBlock API: Non-personal video IDs are queried from the community-driven SponsorBlock database (sponsor.ajay.app) to automate skip segments.
  • Update Checks: The app queries a public GitHub file to notify users when official updates are available.

3. Android Permissions Required

  • INTERNET: Essential for loading search results and streaming video/audio streams.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to maintain uninterrupted background audio playback and status notifications.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to display media player controls in the system notification drawer.
  • REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES: Only requested when the user voluntarily downloads an official app update.

4. Children’s Privacy

Phantom does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

5. Security

Because no user account or personal data is collected on central servers, user privacy is safeguarded by Android OS sandboxing and end-to-end HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.
